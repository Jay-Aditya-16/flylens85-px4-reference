## Fit and application issue

The FlyLens 85 is a 2-inch frame, while the motor's published application is 2.5–4 inches. Flywoo's frame pages also contain revision-dependent/conflicting motor-pattern descriptions. No primary-source curve validates QPT 1404.5 KV4500 with a specific 2-inch prop and this 3S battery.

Required work:

1. Measure frame and motor hole-center geometry, hole/thread diameter, base clearance, shaft/hub fit, and safe screw length.
2. Identify an exact propeller that clears the ducts throughout deformation and does not overload the motor.
3. Test one motor on a guarded thrust stand at representative 3S voltages.
4. Capture thrust, voltage, current, RPM if available, command, vibration, winding/ESC temperature, and duration.
5. Repeat integrated tests for four-channel imbalance, battery sag, desync, and structural resonance.

Do not extrapolate a four-motor vehicle thrust by simply multiplying a single headline maximum; duct interaction, battery sag, thermal limits, and control reserve matter.
